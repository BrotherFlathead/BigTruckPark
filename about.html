About BigTruckPark AutoPost

Empowering creators with intelligent automation.

Our Mission

To democratize social media growth by providing creators, small businesses, and brands with enterprise-grade AI automation tools that are accessible, secure, and easy to use.

Our Story

BigTruckPark AutoPost was born from a simple observation: content creators spend too much time on repetitive publishing tasks and not enough on creating great content.

Our founder, a content creator and software engineer, experienced firsthand the pain of manually scheduling posts, crafting titles, researching hashtags, and analyzing performance across multiple platforms. Each platform required its own workflow, its own tools, its own login — it was unsustainable.

In 2025, we started building what would become BigTruckPark AutoPost: a unified platform that uses AI to handle the entire publishing workflow — from generating optimized titles and hashtags to scheduling posts at the perfect time and providing actionable performance analytics.

Today, BigTruckPark AutoPost helps creators save hours every week, allowing them to focus on what matters most: creating content that resonates with their audience.
