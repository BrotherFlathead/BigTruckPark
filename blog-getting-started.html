Getting Started with BigTruckPark AutoPost

Published: May 05, 2026 · 3 min read

Welcome to BigTruckPark AutoPost! This guide will walk you through the initial setup process and help you get your first automated post up and running.

Step 1: Create Your Account

Visit our homepage and click "Get Started". You'll need to provide your email address and create a secure password. We support OAuth-based authentication, so your password is never stored on our servers.

Step 2: Connect Your Social Accounts

Navigate to the "Accounts" section in your dashboard. Click "Connect Account" and follow the OAuth authorization flow. You'll be redirected to the platform's authorization page — review the permissions and click "Authorize".

Tip: Make sure you're logged into the correct social media account before authorizing. You can connect multiple accounts and manage them all from one dashboard.

Step 3: Configure Your Preferences

Set your preferred posting schedule, default hashtags, and brand voice. Our AI will use these preferences to generate content that matches your style.

  • Posting Schedule: Choose daily, weekly, or custom intervals
  • Brand Voice: Professional, Casual, Humorous, or Inspirational
  • Content Categories: Select topics relevant to your audience

Step 4: Upload Your First Video

Upload a video through the dashboard. Our system will analyze the content and suggest optimized titles, descriptions, and hashtags. You can edit these before scheduling.

Step 5: Review and Launch

Before going fully automated, we recommend using the "Review & Approve" mode for your first few posts. This lets you see what the AI generates and make adjustments before publishing.

What's Next?

Explore the analytics dashboard to track your content's performance. Use the insights to refine your strategy and grow your audience.
