FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about BigTruckPark AutoPost.

▶ What social media platforms do you support?
We currently support posting through official Content Posting APIs. Support for additional platforms is being added regularly based on user demand.
▶ How does the AI generate titles and hashtags?
Our AI analyzes video content, metadata, and trending patterns to generate contextually relevant titles and hashtags optimized for maximum engagement. You can always review and edit before posting.
▶ Is my data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use OAuth for authentication and never store passwords. Access tokens are securely encrypted. We do not share or sell your data.
▶ Can I review content before it gets posted?
Yes. You have full control over the publishing workflow. You can choose between fully automated publishing or review-approve workflow where you review AI-generated content before it goes live.
▶ What kind of analytics do you provide?
We track views, likes, comments, shares, follower growth, engagement rates, and optimal posting times. All data is presented in an easy-to-understand dashboard with trend analysis.
