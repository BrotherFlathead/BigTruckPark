Pricing FAQ

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime, and your access continues through the end of the billing period.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
Yes! All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal. Enterprise plans also support invoicing.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits?
Yes! We offer a 50% discount for verified non-profit organizations. Contact our sales team for details.
